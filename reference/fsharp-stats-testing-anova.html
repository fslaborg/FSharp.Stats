Returns: OneWayAnovaVariationSources

Calculates one-way analysis of variance (one-way ANOVA) which is a technique used to compare means of two or more samples (using the F distribution) The ANOVA tests the null hypothesis that samples in two or more groups are drawn from populations with the same mean values.
